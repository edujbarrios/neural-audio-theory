# Music Producer: From Idea to Approved Track

**Choose this path if your first responsibility is the song.** You decide the brief, references, structure, performance, sonic identity and final creative approval. The separate **[Sound Engineering path](../audio-engineering/index.md)** covers corrective processing, loudness, mastering and file-level verification.

## What you will build

| Stage | Your task | A usable output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Brief | Define audience, reference mood, tempo range, instrumentation and vocal direction | One-page musical brief |
| 2. Generate | Write a constrained prompt; compare candidates using the same criteria | Shortlist of 2–3 viable takes |
| 3. Edit | Correct structure, transitions and performances; replace weak layers where permitted | Approved arrangement |
| 4. Produce | Add recorded instruments or stems, automation, transitions and song dynamics | Producer-approved mix direction |
| 5. Handoff | Mark creative priorities and problems that require an engineer | Approved mix, references, notes and source records |

## Learn in the order you need

### Stage 1 — Understand and direct

1. **[AI Music in Plain Language](../for-dummies.md)** — grasp what generative tools do and their limitations.
2. **[Prompt Engineering Guide](../suno-prompting-guide.md)** — describe structure, production intent, instruments and vocal approach.
3. **[Genre-Specific Prompting](../producer-handbook/genre-specific-prompting.md)** — translate a genre reference into testable instructions.

### Stage 2 — Produce and troubleshoot

4. **[Production Workflow](../producer-handbook/production-workflow.md)** — brief, candidate review, arrangement, revisions and handoff.
5. **[Troubleshooting Generation](../producer-handbook/troubleshooting-generation.md)** — isolate one weakness rather than endlessly regenerating.
6. **[Vocal Synthesis](../producer-handbook/vocal-synthesis.md)** — identify intelligibility, phrasing and identity/consent issues.

### Stage 3 — Finish with an engineer

7. **[Creative Mixing AI Outputs](../producer-handbook/mixing-ai-outputs.md)** — improve focus, emotion and musical contrast.
8. **[DAW Integration](../tools/daw-integration.md)** and **[Stem Separation](../producer-handbook/stem-separation.md)** — understand limits of separated parts and handoffs.
9. **[Mastering for Streaming](../audio-engineering/mastering-for-streaming.md)** — use actual platform measurements rather than loudness myths.
10. **[Quality Control and Delivery](../producer-handbook/quality-control-and-delivery.md)** and **[AI Provenance and Release](../audio-engineering/provenance-and-release.md)** — validate exports and document rights and credits.

| Deliver a master for Spotify | [Mastering for Streaming](../audio-engineering/mastering-for-streaming.md) |

**Producer vs engineer:** You can do both jobs, but separate *creative choices* from *technical acceptance tests*. Neither a specific EQ preset nor a loudness number changes the origin or licensing status of generated audio.
